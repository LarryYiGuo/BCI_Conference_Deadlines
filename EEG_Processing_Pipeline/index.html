Processing Pipeline · P300

脑电处理管线EEG processing pipeline

屏幕上依次闪过一串刺激，五个里有一个是目标。看到目标时，大脑会在约 300 毫秒后多出一个正波，叫 P300。A stream of stimuli flashes on screen, and one in five is a target. When a target appears, the brain adds a positive wave about 300 ms later, called the P300.

这一页把一段带眨眼的脑电，一步步变成「这一次是不是目标」的判断。数据在浏览器里现场合成；任何一步都可以拿掉，看分数怎么变。This page turns a stretch of EEG with blinks in it, step by step, into one decision: was this a target? The data are generated in your browser, and you can take out any step to see how the score changes.

① 滤波① Filtering
P300 的能量主要在 1–10 Hz。带通把更慢的漂移、更快的 α 波和肌电、50 Hz 工频都挡在外面。Most of the P300’s energy is in 1–10 Hz. The band-pass keeps out slower drift, faster alpha and muscle activity, and 50 Hz mains.
幅频响应 · 前向加反向合起来Magnitude response · forward and backward passes combined 两端截止处是 −6 dB−6 dB at both cutoffs
细节Details

零相位。普通滤波器会把波形往后推。先正着滤一遍，再倒着滤一遍，两次的推移抵消，P300 的时间不会变。代价是要用到「未来」的数据，只能离线用；在线系统只能用只看过去的滤波器，接受一点延迟。Zero phase. An ordinary filter shifts the waveform later. Filter forwards, then backwards, and the two shifts cancel, so the P300 stays where it was. The price is that it needs “future” samples, so it only works offline; an online system must use a filter that only looks back, and accept some delay.

高通为什么是 1 Hz。高通越高，慢的正波被削得越多，还会在前后造出本来没有的负波。ERP 研究一般用 0.1 Hz 左右（Tanner 等 2015）；BCI 分类更看重稳定，常用 0.5–1 Hz。Why a 1 Hz high-pass. The higher the high-pass, the more of the slow positive wave it cuts, and it adds negative waves around it that were never there. ERP research usually uses about 0.1 Hz (Tanner et al. 2015); BCI classification cares more about stability and often uses 0.5–1 Hz.

对比：PCAFor contrast: PCA
PCA 找出数据里方差最大的方向，不看标签。它不在这条管线里，放在这里是为了和 ICA 对比。PCA finds the directions of largest variance and ignores the labels. It is not part of the pipeline; it is here for contrast with ICA.
PC1 的头皮分布PC1 scalp map
8 个主成分各占的方差Variance share of the 8 components

所以不能直接砍掉 PC1：眨眼和额区脑电在空间上有重叠，会被一起砍掉。So you cannot simply cut PC1: blinks and frontal EEG overlap in space, and both would go.

PCA 更常用在 ICA 的第一步，叫白化：把数据旋转再缩放，让每个方向的方差相等、互不相关。PCA is more often ICA’s first step, called whitening: rotate and rescale the data so every direction has the same variance and no two are correlated.

② ICA 去眨眼② ICA: removing blinks
每个电极录到的都是几个来源的混合：眨眼、各处的脑电、肌电。ICA 把它们拆开，然后删掉眨眼那一个。Each electrode records a mix of sources: blinks, brain activity, muscle. ICA separates them, and the blink source is dropped.

眨眼又高又尖、偶尔才出现，最不像正态分布，所以最容易被认出来。下面 8 张卡片按这个「不像正态」的程度（峰度）排好了。A blink is tall, sharp and occasional, so it looks least like a normal distribution and is the easiest source to spot. The 8 cards below are sorted by how un-normal each one looks (kurtosis).

细节Details

A 的每一列是一个来源在 8 个电极上的分布，W 把混合拆回来。ICA 假设各来源相互独立、混合方式整段不变。Each column of A is one source’s scalp map, and W undoes the mixing. ICA assumes the sources are independent and the mixing stays fixed for the whole recording.

许多独立来源加在一起会趋向正态，所以越不像正态的方向，越可能是单独一个来源。这里用超额峰度，正态分布为 0。Many independent sources added together tend towards a normal distribution, so the less normal a direction looks, the more likely it is a single source. Kurtosis here is excess kurtosis, which is 0 for a normal distribution.

细节Details

降采样之前必须先低通，否则高于 12.5 Hz 的成分会混叠进低频。前面的 10 Hz 低通已经做了这件事。Low-pass filtering must come before downsampling; otherwise anything above 12.5 Hz aliases into the low frequencies. The 10 Hz low-pass earlier already did this.

真实的 P300 在头顶偏后的 Pz 最大。这 8 个电极里没有 Pz，离它最近的是 C3 和 C4。A real P300 is largest at Pz, on the midline just behind the top of the head. These 8 electrodes have no Pz; the closest are C3 and C4.

④ xDAWN 空间滤波④ xDAWN spatial filtering
ICA 不知道哪些试次是目标，xDAWN 知道。它找一组电极权重，让合成出的虚拟通道里，目标响应占的比例最大。ICA does not know which trials are targets; xDAWN does. It finds electrode weights that give the target response the largest possible share of a virtual channel.

分子 CP 是目标平均波形的能量，分母 CX 是全部数据的波动。取比值最大的 2 个方向，每个试次就从 160 个数降到 2 × 20 = 40 个。The top, CP, is the energy of the average target waveform; the bottom, CX, is the total fluctuation of the data. Keep the 2 directions with the largest ratio, and each trial shrinks from 160 numbers to 2 × 20 = 40.

它用了标签，所以交叉验证的每一折都要只用训练数据重新拟合。下面的图用全部试次拟合，只作展示。It uses the labels, so in cross-validation it is refitted on the training data in every fold. The plots below are fitted on all trials, for illustration only.

细节Details

权重不等于分布。滤波器常给没有信号的电极一个负权重，用来减掉各电极共有的背景噪声。要回答「信号在哪儿」，看空间分布（pattern），不看权重（Haufe 等 2014）。Weights are not patterns. A filter often gives a negative weight to an electrode with no signal, to subtract the background noise the electrodes share. To ask “where is the signal”, look at the spatial pattern, not the weights (Haufe et al. 2014).

ICA 之后的坑。删掉一个成分后，8 个电极只剩 7 个独立维度，CX 不再可逆。pyRiemann 的 Xdawn 默认会报错；改用 estimator='lwf' 能跑，但第一个滤波器正好指向被删掉的方向，输出全是 0，而且不报错。A trap after ICA. Once a component is removed, the 8 electrodes have only 7 independent dimensions and CX is no longer invertible. pyRiemann’s Xdawn fails by default; with estimator='lwf' it runs, but the first filter points exactly at the removed direction and outputs all zeros, with no error.

数学上这是一个广义特征值问题。这一页只在方差不为零的方向里求解；用 MNE 时，直接把 ICA 留下的 7 个成分当输入就行（见下面的代码），两种做法结果相同。Mathematically this is a generalised eigenvalue problem. This page solves it only in the directions with non-zero variance; with MNE, simply feed in the 7 components ICA keeps (see the code below). The two give the same result.

⑤ LDA 分类⑤ LDA classification
LDA 给每个特征一个权重，加起来是一个分数，大于 0 判为目标。权重不只看两类差多少，还要算上噪声之间的相关。LDA gives each feature a weight and adds them into one score; above 0 means target. The weights depend not only on how much the classes differ, but also on how the noise is correlated.

评估Evaluation
数据分成 5 份，轮流拿 1 份测试、4 份训练。用到标签的 xDAWN 和 LDA，只在训练的 4 份上拟合。The data are split into 5 folds; each takes a turn as the test set while the other 4 train. xDAWN and LDA, which use labels, are fitted on the 4 training folds only.

目标只占 20%，全猜「非目标」准确率就有 80%，所以不看准确率。AUC 是随机挑一个目标、一个非目标，目标分数更高的概率；0.5 等于瞎猜。平衡准确率（BACC）是两类各自答对比例的平均。Targets are only 20% of trials, so always guessing “non-target” already gives 80% accuracy; that is why accuracy is not used. AUC is the chance that a random target scores higher than a random non-target; 0.5 is guessing. Balanced accuracy (BACC) is the average of the fraction correct in each class.

消融：每次拿掉一步Ablations: one step out at a time
打乱标签：泄漏有多大Shuffled labels: how big is the leak

把标签随机打乱，AUC 就应该是 0.5。打乱 20 次，比较四种做法。「泄漏」指一个用到标签的步骤看到了测试数据。Shuffle the labels at random and the AUC should be 0.5. Shuffle 20 times and compare four procedures. A “leak” means a step that uses labels got to see the test data.

同一条管线用 MNE、pyRiemann、scikit-learn 写出来是这样。xDAWN 和 LDA 放进同一个 Pipeline 交给 cross_val_score，每一折就只用训练数据拟合。The same pipeline in MNE, pyRiemann and scikit-learn. Put xDAWN and LDA in one Pipeline and hand it to cross_val_score, and each fold fits them on the training data alone.
import mne
from mne.preprocessing import ICA
from pyriemann.spatialfilters import Xdawn
from sklearn.pipeline import make_pipeline
from sklearn.preprocessing import FunctionTransformer
from sklearn.discriminant_analysis import LinearDiscriminantAnalysis as LDA
from sklearn.model_selection import StratifiedKFold, cross_val_score

# raw: mne.io.Raw，8 导，250 Hz
# events: (n, 3) 的整数数组，第 3 列 1 = 非目标，2 = 目标
raw.notch_filter(50, method='iir')
raw.filter(1, 10, method='iir')                 # 默认零相位：正着一遍、倒着一遍

ica = ICA(n_components=8, method='fastica', random_state=0).fit(raw)
ica.exclude, _ = ica.find_bads_eog(raw, ch_name='Fp1',     # 用 Fp1 当眼电参考
                                   measure='correlation', threshold=0.8)
src = ica.get_sources(raw)                                  # 8 个成分的时间序列
src.drop_channels([src.ch_names[i] for i in ica.exclude])   # 删掉眨眼成分，剩 7 个，满秩

epochs = mne.Epochs(src, events, event_id={'nontarget': 1, 'target': 2},
                    tmin=0, tmax=0.8, baseline=None, decim=10,
                    picks='all', preload=True)
X, y = epochs.get_data(), epochs.events[:, 2]

clf = make_pipeline(
    Xdawn(nfilter=2, classes=[2]),
    FunctionTransformer(lambda Z: Z.reshape(len(Z), -1)),
    LDA(solver='lsqr', shrinkage='auto'))
cv = StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=0)
print(cross_val_score(clf, X, y, cv=cv, scoring='roc_auc').mean())
import mne
from mne.preprocessing import ICA
from pyriemann.spatialfilters import Xdawn
from sklearn.pipeline import make_pipeline
from sklearn.preprocessing import FunctionTransformer
from sklearn.discriminant_analysis import LinearDiscriminantAnalysis as LDA
from sklearn.model_selection import StratifiedKFold, cross_val_score

# raw: mne.io.Raw, 8 channels, 250 Hz
# events: (n, 3) integer array; column 3 is 1 = non-target, 2 = target
raw.notch_filter(50, method='iir')
raw.filter(1, 10, method='iir')                 # zero phase by default: forwards, then backwards

ica = ICA(n_components=8, method='fastica', random_state=0).fit(raw)
ica.exclude, _ = ica.find_bads_eog(raw, ch_name='Fp1',     # use Fp1 as the EOG reference
                                   measure='correlation', threshold=0.8)
src = ica.get_sources(raw)                                  # time courses of the 8 components
src.drop_channels([src.ch_names[i] for i in ica.exclude])   # drop the blink component: 7 left, full rank

epochs = mne.Epochs(src, events, event_id={'nontarget': 1, 'target': 2},
                    tmin=0, tmax=0.8, baseline=None, decim=10,
                    picks='all', preload=True)
X, y = epochs.get_data(), epochs.events[:, 2]

clf = make_pipeline(
    Xdawn(nfilter=2, classes=[2]),
    FunctionTransformer(lambda Z: Z.reshape(len(Z), -1)),
    LDA(solver='lsqr', shrinkage='auto'))
cv = StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=0)
print(cross_val_score(clf, X, y, cv=cv, scoring='roc_auc').mean())
细节Details

find_bads_eog 为什么不用默认阈值。默认把 8 个相关系数换成 z 分数，超过 3 才算眼电成分。可 8 个数里最大的 z 分数最多 √7 ≈ 2.65，所以默认设置在 8 导数据上永远挑不出来。通道少时，直接用相关系数做阈值。Why not find_bads_eog’s default threshold. By default the 8 correlations become z-scores, and only z above 3 counts. But the largest of 8 numbers can reach at most √7 ≈ 2.65, so on 8 channels the default never picks anything. With few channels, threshold on the correlation itself.

为什么不用 ica.apply。它把眨眼从 8 个电极里减掉，得到的数据只有 7 维，xDAWN 会撞上前面那个坑。直接用 7 个保留成分可以绕开。Why not ica.apply. It subtracts the blink from the 8 electrodes, leaving 7-dimensional data, and xDAWN walks into the trap above. Using the 7 kept components avoids it.

这段代码在页面第一次打开时的那批数据上测过（MNE 1.12、pyRiemann 0.11、scikit-learn 1.9）：AUC 0.84，nfilter=1 时 0.89；页面上是 0.84 和 0.88。剩下的差别来自滤波器设计、ICA 实现和分折方式。Tested on the batch this page shows when it first opens (MNE 1.12, pyRiemann 0.11, scikit-learn 1.9): AUC 0.84, or 0.89 with nfilter=1; the page gives 0.84 and 0.88. The rest of the gap comes from filter design, the ICA implementation and how the folds are split.

FastICA 没收敛时，不同浏览器的数学函数在最后几位上的差别会被迭代放大，结果可能在小数点后第三位上略有不同。When FastICA has not converged, tiny last-digit differences between browsers’ built-in mathematical functions are amplified by the iterations, so results can differ slightly in the third decimal place.
