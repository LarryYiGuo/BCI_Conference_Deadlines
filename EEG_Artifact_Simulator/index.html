02 / 04 · Artifact Removal

怎么去掉伪迹Removing artifacts

伪迹清单Artifact list
伪迹能不能靠滤波去掉，只看一件事：它的频率和脑电重不重叠。只有工频和很慢的漂移不重叠，其余的要靠 ICA 或直接剔除。Whether filtering can remove an artifact depends on one thing: does its frequency overlap with EEG? Only mains noise and very slow drift do not; everything else needs ICA or rejection.
三种滤波设置Three filter settings3
三种都是因果滤波，只用过去的数据，和在线系统一样。离线分析时 MNE 默认用零相位滤波，所以看到的伪迹形状会略有不同。All three are causal filters that use only past data, as an online system must. Offline, MNE filters with zero phase by default, so artifact shapes look slightly different.
眼动伪迹怎么处理Handling eye artifacts
先看实验对眼睛的要求。盯住注视点时，眼动是噪声，要去掉；自由看时，眼动是任务的一部分，要建模；在线闭环还只能用已经录到的数据。Start from what the experiment asks of the eyes. With fixation, eye movements are noise to remove; with free viewing they are part of the task and must be modelled; an online closed loop can only use data already recorded.

04 / 04 · Notes & Sources

设计依据与来源Design notes and sources

为什么是这 8 个电极Why these 8 electrodes8
8 是 OpenBCI Cyton 这类入门设备的通道数。每个电极放在某一种信号或伪迹最强的位置。这套布局是为演示伪迹选的：做运动想象会集中在 C3、Cz、C4，做视觉解码会集中在枕叶和顶叶。Eight is the channel count of entry-level boards such as the OpenBCI Cyton. Each electrode sits where one signal or artifact is strongest. The layout is chosen to show artifacts: motor imagery would cluster around C3, Cz and C4, visual decoding over occipital and parietal sites.
幅度校准Amplitude calibration
